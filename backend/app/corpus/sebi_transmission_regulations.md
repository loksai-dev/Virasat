---
Under SEBI regulations and Depository Participant operating guidelines (NSDL/CDSL), where a deceased sole security holder had a registered nomination in their demat account, the securities are transmitted directly to the demat account of the nominee upon submission of the transmission request form (TRF), client master list (CML) of the nominee, and certified death certificate. The Depository Participant is discharged once the units are credited to the nominee's account.

topic: "Transmission of Demat Securities Without Registered Nominee"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.sebi.gov.in"
---
For transmission of securities held in sole name without nomination:
1. For portfolio market valuations up to the SEBI threshold limit (INR 5,00,000 for physical certificates / INR 15,00,000 for demat accounts): transmission is permitted upon joint application by all legal heirs accompanied by a family tree affidavit, indemnity bond with sureties, and no-objection certificates (NOC) from non-claiming heirs.
2. For portfolio market valuations exceeding the threshold limit: production of a court succession certificate, probate of will, or letters of administration is mandatory.

clause_id: SEBI-TRANSMISSION-DOCUMENTS
source: "Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Standardized Transmission Framework"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Standardized Documentation Checklist for Transmission of Shares and Mutual Funds"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.sebi.gov.in"
---
SEBI prescribes standardized formats for transmission across all Registrars and Share Transfer Agents (RTAs) and Depository Participants:
1. Transmission Request Form (TRF) executed by the legal heir or nominee.
2. Original or notarized copy of the death certificate.
3. Self-attested PAN card and Aadhaar KYC of the claimant.
4. Client Master Report (CMR) or Client Master List (CML) showing active demat account details.
5. In non-nominee claims: Affidavit from all legal heirs, Indemnity Bond executed on non-judicial stamp paper, and NOC or relinquishment deed from legal heirs not claiming transmission.

clause_id: SEBI-TRANSMISSION-TIMELINES

topic: "SLA and Processing Timelines for Securities Transmission"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.sebi.gov.in"
---
Under SEBI guidelines, Depository Participants and RTAs must process transmission requests within 7 working days for demat holdings with nomination upon receipt of complete and defect-free documentation. In non-nominee claims requiring scrutiny of legal heirship, the SLA is within 21 calendar days, subject to resolution of any deficiencies.
