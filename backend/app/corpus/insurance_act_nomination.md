clause_id: INS-ACT-NOMINATION
source: "Insurance Act, 1938"
section: "39"
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Nomination Facility in Life Insurance Policies"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.irdai.gov.in"
---
Under Section 39 of the Insurance Act, 1938, the holder of a policy of life insurance may, when effecting the policy or at any time before policy maturity, nominate the person or persons to whom the money secured by the policy shall be paid in the event of their death. The insurer is discharged of liability upon paying the policy proceeds to the registered nominee.

clause_id: INS-BENEFICIAL-NOMINEE
source: "Insurance Act, 1938"
section: "39"
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Beneficial Nominee Status for Spouse, Children, and Parents"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.irdai.gov.in"
---
Under amendments to Section 39(7) of the Insurance Act, 1938, where a nominee is appointed who is the parent, or the spouse, or the children of the policyholder, such nominee becomes a 'beneficial nominee'. Unlike general bank nominees who act purely as trustees, beneficial nominees take absolute beneficial ownership in the policy sum assured to the exclusion of other legal heirs or creditors of the deceased life assured, unless an adverse charge was lawfully created.

clause_id: INS-CLAIM-DOCUMENTS
source: "Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) Guidelines"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Death Claim Documentation for Life Insurance Settlement"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.irdai.gov.in"
---
Settlement of life insurance death claims requires submission of:
1. Formally executed insurer claim intimation form signed by the nominee or legal heir.
2. Original policy document bond.
3. Official death certificate issued by the municipal or local government registrar.
4. KYC verification proofs (PAN, Aadhaar/Voter ID) and cancelled cheque of the claimant's bank account for electronic fund transfer.
5. In early death claims (within 3 years of policy inception): medical records, hospital treatment certificates, and attending physician statements.

clause_id: INS-CLAIM-TIMELINES
source: "Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) Protection of Policyholders' Interests Regulations"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Turnaround Time and Interest on Delayed Insurance Claim Settlement"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.irdai.gov.in"
---
Under IRDAI regulations, an insurer must settle a death claim where no investigation is warranted within 30 days from the date of receipt of all required documents. Where claim investigation is required, the insurer must initiate the inquiry immediately and conclude the assessment within 90 days. If payment is delayed beyond the statutory timeline, the insurer is liable to pay interest to the claimant at the bank rate plus two percent from the due date until actual remittance.
