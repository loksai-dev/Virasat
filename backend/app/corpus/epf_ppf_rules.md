needs_review: true

---
Under paragraph 70 of the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952, on the death of a member, the accumulations standing to their credit in the Fund are payable to the registered nominee or nominees in the proportions specified. If a member had a family at the time of nomination, any nomination in favour of a non-family member is invalid. Where no valid nomination exists, the amount is divided equally among surviving family members (spouse, minor children, dependent parents).

section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Insurance Benefit Claim Under the EDLI Scheme on Death of Member"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.epfindia.gov.in"
---
Under the Employees' Deposit-Linked Insurance (EDLI) Scheme, 1976, if an employee covered by EPF dies while in active employment service, the registered nominee (or eligible family members in absence of nomination) is entitled to an assurance benefit calculated on the average monthly wages and provident fund balance, subject to the statutory minimum and maximum caps. Claims are submitted using composite claim forms or Form 5IF accompanied by employer attestation and death certificate.

clause_id: PPF-NOMINATION-CLAIM
source: "Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "PPF Account Closure and Payment to Registered Nominee"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.nsiindia.gov.in"
---
Under the Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019 (notified under the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873), on the death of a PPF account holder, the balance cannot be continued by contributions. The account must be closed and the entire balance paid to the registered nominee or nominees upon submission of the prescribed claim application (Form G / Form 11), death certificate, and passbook. The nominee holds the funds as trustee for the benefit of the legal heirs of the subscriber.

clause_id: PPF-DECEASED-NO-NOMINEE

section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "PPF Claim Settlement Where No Nomination Exists"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.nsiindia.gov.in"
---
Where a PPF account holder dies intestate without a registered nomination:
1. For balances up to the statutory limit (INR 5,00,000): payment is made to the legal heirs upon submission of a joint claim application, death certificate, legal heirship certificate or family tree affidavit, consent of all heirs, and an indemnity bond with suitable solvent sureties.
2. For balances exceeding the limit: production of a succession certificate issued by a competent civil court or letters of administration is mandatory before the post office or bank releases the funds.
