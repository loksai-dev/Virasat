---
Mutation of immovable property refers to the administrative recording of change in title or ownership in the revenue and municipal property tax records. On the death of an owner intestate, mutation in favour of legal heirs requires submission of the registered title deed, death certificate, legal heir certificate or surviving member certificate from the revenue authority, family genealogy affidavit, property tax clearance receipt, and published public notice invite without objections. Mutation updates fiscal liability for municipal property taxes but does not by itself confer judicial title.

topic: "Property Mutation Based on Registered Testamentary Instrument"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.indiacode.nic.in"
---
Where mutation of immovable property is sought on the basis of a registered will executed by the deceased testator:
1. The applicant legatee must furnish the registered original will, death certificate of the testator, encumbrance certificate, and affidavit confirming that the will was the last unrevoked testamentary disposition.
2. In non-presidency areas where probate is not compulsory for Hindu wills, municipal and revenue authorities effect mutation following a statutory notice period (typically 30 days) if no rival heir lodges a caveat or dispute. If disputed, mutation is deferred until probate or civil court declaration.

jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Transfer of Motor Vehicle Ownership on Demise of Registered Owner"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1798"
---
Under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (Section 56) and Rule 56 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, when the registered owner of a motor vehicle dies, the person succeeding to the ownership must inform the registering authority of the demise within 30 days and apply for transfer of ownership to their name within three months. The vehicle may be used for up to three months by the successor upon due intimation.

topic: "RTO Formalities and Documentation for Vehicle Inheritance"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1798"
---
To effect transfer of vehicle registration to an heir at the Regional Transport Office (RTO), the following documents must be submitted:
1. Form 31 (Application for transfer of ownership on death) executed by the successor.
2. Original Certificate of Registration (RC book or smart card).
3. Death certificate of the registered owner.
4. Valid vehicle insurance certificate and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate.
5. No Objection Certificate (NOC) and consent affidavits from all other surviving legal heirs relinquishing rights in the motor vehicle in favour of the applicant heir.
