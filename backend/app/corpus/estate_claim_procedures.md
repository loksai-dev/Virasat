---
A Legal Heir Certificate (also termed Surviving Member Certificate in certain states) is an administrative document issued by the local revenue authority (such as Tahsildar, Mandal Revenue Officer, or Revenue Divisional Officer) following local inquiry. It identifies the surviving immediate family members (spouse, children, parents) of a deceased person. It is widely accepted by banks, insurers, and government departments for processing claims of movable assets, gratuity, and pension where no formal civil court succession certificate is insisted upon.

needs_review: true

---
An Indemnity Bond is an undertaking executed on non-judicial stamp paper by the claimants (and often one or two independent solvent third-party sureties) in favour of a financial institution. The claimants covenant to keep the institution harmless and indemnified against any loss, cost, damages, or adverse claims raised in the future by any undisclosed heir, creditor, or claimant in respect of the released estate funds or securities.

source: "Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "Official Death Certificate Verification and Vital Statistics"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1545"
---
Under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969, an official death certificate issued by the Registrar of Births and Deaths (Municipal Corporation, Municipality, or Gram Panchayat) constitutes primary legal proof of the demise of a person, the exact date of death, place of death, and registration details. All statutory and institutional claim procedures require the production of an authenticated copy of this certificate before succession proceedings, asset releases, or transfers can commence.

topic: "Standard Claim Application Forms Across BFSI Institutions"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.rbi.org.in"
---
A deceased claim application form is the standardized institutional document through which a nominee, joint survivor, or legal heirs formally request the closure or release of accounts, deposits, or safe deposit lockers. The form captures:
1. Deceased customer details, account numbers, and death particulars.
2. Claimant details, relationship to deceased, and capacity of claim (nominee, legal heir, executor).
3. Declaration of other surviving family members and undertakings regarding the absence of conflicting litigations or injunctions.

topic: "Bank Account Verification and Electronic Remittance Formalities"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.rbi.org.in"
---
To prevent misdirection of estate distributions and satisfy statutory anti-money laundering and KYC norms, financial institutions require claimants to submit:
1. An officially verified copy of their Permanent Account Number (PAN) card and proof of address (Aadhaar, passport, or voter card).
2. A personalized cancelled cheque leaf or certified bank statement reflecting the claimant's account name, account number, and IFSC code to facilitate direct credit via NEFT/RTGS. Payments in cash or third-party endorsements are strictly prohibited.

clause_id: PROCESS-AFFIDAVIT-NO-DISPUTE
source: "Standard Estate Administration Practice"
section: ""
jurisdiction: "India"
topic: "No-Objection Affidavit and Relinquishment Formalities by Legal Heirs"
needs_review: true
official_url: "https://www.rbi.org.in"
---
Where multiple legal heirs are entitled to succeed to an intestate's asset but agree that one specific heir shall receive the proceeds on behalf of all or for convenience of settlement, the remaining heirs must execute a notarized No Objection Certificate (NOC) or relinquishment affidavit. The affidavit explicitly confirms that the deponents have no objection to the release of the asset to the nominated applicant and that payment to such applicant constitutes valid discharge of the institution.
